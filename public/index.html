Weather & air

National Weather Service API ↗Forecasts, alerts, and mountain stations for the smog lid
Utah DAQ air monitors ↗Live PM2.5 and AQI across the Wasatch Front
EPA AQS ↗Historical PM2.5 behind the outdoor-comfort estimate
Open-Meteo ↗Snow and mountain forecasts for ski resorts

Getting around

UDOT Traffic API ↗Road conditions, closures, canyon roads
UTA GTFS-Realtime ↗TRAX, bus, and FrontRunner service alerts

Places & outdoors

UGRC Utah Trailheads ↗Official state trailhead and trail data
Recreation.gov RIDB ↗Campgrounds and recreation facilities
OpenStreetMap contributors ↗Cafés, food, and community places (ODbL)

Events

Ticketmaster Discovery API ↗Concerts, games, and ticketed shows

Maps & AI

MapTiler ↗Street map, terrain, and contour lines
OpenFreeMap ↗Advanced map basemap (OSM data)
AWS Terrain Tiles ↗Elevation for 3D terrain and the smog layer
OpenAI ↗Ask YOUtah answers, grounded in the sources above
